Frequently asked questions

How does SwipeRoll work?

Swipe through your photos and videos like a deck of cards. Swipe left to queue an item for deletion, or right to keep it. Nothing is deleted until you review and confirm everything at the end of a session.

Are my photos ever uploaded or shared?

No. SwipeRoll runs entirely on your device. Your library is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone — including us. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Will I lose photos by accident?

No item is removed until you tap "Confirm delete" on the summary screen, and iOS shows its own confirmation before anything is deleted. Deleted photos also go to your Recently Deleted album for 30 days, so you can recover them if needed.

Why don't photos I've kept show up again?

SwipeRoll remembers what you've already reviewed so you don't see the same photos twice. When you finish, tap "Sort entire library again" to reset this and review your whole library from scratch.

SwipeRoll only shows some of my photos.

This usually means SwipeRoll was granted access to only selected photos. To give access to your full library, open the Settings app → SwipeRoll → Photos, and choose "Full Access".
